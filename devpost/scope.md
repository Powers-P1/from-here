# FROM HERE

Turn one dense notice into a clearer A3 sign while preserving its important facts.

## The Unique Kernel
The AI plans a sign using human-confirmed facts. A fresh model call reads the
actual rendered sign without the original or expected answers. The agent can
repair the layout in response to measured checks, up to twice, or stop honestly.
Facts include conditions and exceptions, not only matching isolated words.

## Who It's For
A school office or reception worker preparing a time-and-place notice. People
need the right action, location and time; some need a different instruction under
a condition, such as wheelchair-accessible pickup at another entrance.

## The Core Loop
Add an English PNG/JPG, choose a target viewing distance, confirm the extracted
details, let the agent design and check the sign, compare, download PDF or SVG.
Start a new notice with an explicit discard confirmation. Three main tasks:
add the source, confirm the facts, get the result.

## Inspiration & Identity
Participant-approved ImageGen views: white canvas, dark ink, signal yellow,
strong condensed sign typography, quiet links and open rows. One clear action.
The app's own hierarchy must communicate as clearly as the signs it creates.
No generic AI chat, ornamental dashboard or competing cards.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The participant wants a useful, memorable hackathon entry and explicitly values
clarity, honest verification and restraint over extra features. No personal
market experience or user research is claimed.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A newly supplied notice becomes an actual downloadable A3 SVG/PDF. Its audience,
action, destination, date/time qualifier and exception remain correct. An obscured
time is left unknown until confirmed. The report and repair history reflect real
execution; automatic checks do not certify how humans will understand a sign.

## The POC Boundary
One current English notice. A3 portrait. Target distance 1, 3 or 5 m. Editable
confirmed facts, bounded AI layout, checks and at most two repairs, comparison,
PDF/SVG, recoverable errors and optional two-second memory exercise. The approved
32 visual states share eight UI families; they are not 32 mandatory steps.

## Later
Broader layouts and languages require separate evaluation. Optional hosting
comes after the local complete flow and does not replace a recording or repository.

## Explicitly Cut
Accounts and project library: not needed for a single notice.
Full graphics editor and printer integration: not needed to prove the kernel.
Translations, purchases and auto-publication: outside this experiment.
Claims of certified accessibility, guaranteed reading distance or measured business
savings: unsupported by this proof of concept.
