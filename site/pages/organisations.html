One instruction to get right.

A dense paragraph is easy to overlook at a busy doorway. Reformatting it by hand also creates another chance to lose a time, place or exception.

FROM HERE gives you a bounded workflow: extract the facts, confirm them, build a sign and read it back from the finished image. A failed check leads to a repair or an honest stop.

Before you print.

  1. Confirm the instruction.

    Check the source photo and edit anything AI has read incorrectly. Missing facts are not permission to invent an answer.

  2. Keep the exception connected.

    “Wheelchair-accessible pickup” and “Main Entrance” belong together. The destination alone is not enough.

  3. Check it with real readers.

    Print at Actual size on A3 and test the sign in its setting. A target viewing distance is a design input, not a guarantee.

A practical business question.

Can clearer signs reduce repeated directions and manual reformatting? That is the value to test with a team using real notices.
