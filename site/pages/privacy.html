Your photo. A temporary job.

What happens when you try this public demo.

Photo and AI processing

The current app sends your notice image and text to Anthropic for Claude Sonnet 5.5 processing. The provider key stays on the server; visitors do not supply one.

Use the synthetic sample if you only want to evaluate the workflow. Only upload material you are allowed to process, and avoid personal information in a public demo.

Temporary, not a project library

Jobs and images are held in application memory. A server restart or capacity cleanup can remove them. Download the PDF or SVG to keep your result.

A signed browser-session cookie controls access to your job’s source and exports. The app has no user account, database or cross-device saved-project list. Changing browser or clearing its cookies can lose access to that job.

What the demo accepts

English PNG or JPG photos up to 10 MB, with one main instruction. Unreadable facts must be resolved before redesign. A viewing-distance setting is a design input.

Uploads, actions and simultaneous AI work have demo limits. If the service is busy, wait and follow its retry message. The app can sleep after inactivity; allow about a minute to restart.

About this website

The public information pages and saved synthetic example are served through GitHub Pages. They make no AI request. Opening the separate app begins the interactive session.

There is no analytics script, advertising pixel or marketing form in these static pages. Hosting providers still handle requests under their own policies.
