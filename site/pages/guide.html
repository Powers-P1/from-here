Your notice. Five real steps.

An optional walkthrough inside the working application. You do the work; the guide follows completed actions.

Allow about a minute on first launch.

The app runs on Render Free, which sleeps after 15 minutes without activity. Keep its new tab open while the server restarts. This website and the saved example do not need that server.

Follow the actual workflow.

  1. Choose a notice.

    Use sample notice opens the synthetic school photo. You can also choose your own English PNG or JPG, up to 10 MB.

  2. Find key details.

    Start the real AI reading. There is no stored answer substituted for this step.

  3. Confirm, then redesign.

    Compare the facts with the original. Check the condition and its instruction together. Edit if needed and choose Confirm & redesign.

  4. Inspect the five readings.

    Open Details on mobile or View checks and changes on desktop. Compare who, what, where, when and the exception.

  5. Download the PDF.

    Return to the result and choose Download PDF. The walkthrough completes after a successful PDF download starts.

Always your actions.

No automatic clicking. No timed “success.” No Next button that skips a failed check.

The guide stays in the page flow, so it does not cover the controls. You can exit it at any point and keep working.

If something stops.

A busy service or interrupted AI call is not a passed result. Follow the app’s retry or review action. If the content check still fails after two layout repairs, the app stops rather than certifying it.

You can keep an explicitly unchecked SVG draft after a content stop. That does not complete the checked-PDF walkthrough. Read the demo limits.
