What to try

  1. Use the sample notice.

    Start with the provided synthetic photo. Sample inputs go through the real pipeline.

  2. Find key details.

    Compare the actual extraction with the source. It must keep the audience, action, location and date/time.

  3. Confirm the pickup exception.

    Wheelchair-accessible pickup remains at the Main Entrance. Check both the condition and its linked instruction, then redesign.

  4. Open the five readings.

    Use Details or View checks and changes. These compare the sign’s AI readback with the facts you confirmed.

  5. Download an A3 PDF.

    Also try the editable SVG. The checked artwork is the artwork used for export.

Where AI earns its place.

  1. PhotoReal uploaded input
  2. FactsAI reads and extracts
  3. Your confirmationReview and edit
  4. Rendered signBounded layout, actual image
  5. Fresh AI readbackRead the finished image

Fresh readback receives the rendered image, not the source or expected answers. Its separate parsing request receives only that reading. A mismatch can trigger up to two layout repairs, followed by a new check. If it still fails, the workflow stops.

Evaluation limits

English, one main instruction, temporary jobs and two concurrent AI workflows. A busy response can require a retry.

AI checks concern content recovery and layout. They do not certify human readability, accessibility or a physical reading distance. No measured commercial ROI is claimed.
